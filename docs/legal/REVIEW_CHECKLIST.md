# Legal and compliance review checklist

Nothing below is complete until supported by written evidence and an identified reviewer.

## Corporate and contacts

- [ ] Legal entity name/type and commercial registration verified
- [ ] Registered and operating addresses verified
- [ ] Support, legal, privacy, and billing channels verified
- [ ] Public phone and business hours approved

## Legal review

- [ ] Qualified Iraqi lawyer reviewed all English and Arabic text
- [ ] Arabic and English legal meaning reconciled; controlling-language rule approved
- [ ] Governing law, forum, consumer rights, and age rule approved
- [ ] Liability, warranties, suspension, termination, and IP clauses approved
- [ ] Electronic contracting and consent mechanism reviewed
- [ ] Privacy roles, legal bases, rights, transfers, cookies, incidents, and retention approved

## Product and commercial facts

- [ ] Product/service description verified against production behavior
- [ ] Trial duration, limits, expiry, licensing, support, builds, and checksums verified
- [ ] Prices, currency, taxes, renewal, cancellation, and refund mechanics verified
- [ ] Payment provider and hosted-checkout/webhook architecture approved
- [ ] All processors, hosting regions, subprocessors, and contracts inventoried

## Versioning and release

- [ ] Each document assigned an immutable approved version
- [ ] Effective and last-updated dates approved
- [ ] Rendered content hash/evidence retained with approval record
- [ ] Frontend and Edge Function version parity tested
- [ ] Registration remains disabled until approved Terms/Privacy are deployed
- [ ] Trial acceptance is collected at download stage only if approved/required
- [ ] Refund acknowledgement is collected at checkout only if approved/required
- [ ] Accessibility, RTL/LTR, print, deep links, and mobile layouts tested
- [ ] Rollback and prior-version archive prepared
